Mobile Tire Change in Oklahoma City & Edmond

Flat tire? We come to you — 24/7. Call (405) 679-6655

A flat tire does not have to leave you stranded. Sawa Towing LLC provides mobile tire-change help in Oklahoma City and Edmond, 24 hours a day. Tell us where you are and whether your vehicle has a usable spare tire, wheel-lock key, and the tools supplied with the vehicle.

We come to your location

We can help remove the flat tire and install your usable spare when the vehicle is in a safe, accessible location. If you are beside fast-moving traffic or in another dangerous spot, move to safety if you can and tell us about the conditions when you call.

When the vehicle may need a tow

A tire change may not be possible when there is no usable spare, the wheel or suspension is damaged, the vehicle cannot be lifted safely, or required hardware is unavailable. In that situation, we can discuss towing the vehicle instead — local towing starts at $80.

What to have ready

  • Your exact location
  • Vehicle year, make, and model
  • Whether a usable spare is available
  • Whether the vehicle has locking lug nuts
