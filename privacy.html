Privacy Policy

How we handle your information when you call, message, or book a tow.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Sawa Towing LLC ("Sawa Towing," "we," "us") provides towing and roadside assistance in Oklahoma City and Edmond, Oklahoma. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you call us, message us, or visit sawatowing.com, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you request service by phone, text message, our website forms, or messaging features on Google Search and Google Maps, we collect your name, phone number, vehicle information, pickup and drop-off locations, and any details you share about the service you need.

Payment information. If you pay by card, payment is processed by our payment provider. We do not store your full card number on our systems.

Website information. Like most websites, our site may collect basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited, and general usage data through cookies or similar tools used by our hosting and analytics providers.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your requests, dispatch a driver, provide quotes, perform the service, send invoices and receipts, communicate with you about your job, and operate and improve our business. We do not sell your personal information.

When We Share Information

We share information only as needed to provide service: with payment processors to complete a transaction; with motor clubs and dispatch networks (such as the roadside program through which you requested service) to complete your job; with repair shops or destinations you direct us to; and when required by law or to protect safety and property.

Messaging

If you contact us through Google Business Profile messages, SMS, or other messaging channels, we receive and store the conversation so we can help you, and automated or AI-assisted replies may be used to answer routine questions. Standard messaging rates from your carrier may apply.

Your Choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by calling us at (405) 679-6655. We keep service records as long as needed for business, tax, and legal purposes.
